1. During a CPU1 interrupt, or syscall (essentially also a interrupt), the hardware will pack the trapframe and push it to the stack, while the CPU0 is still running and having the lock. During this gap time, the CPU1 doesn't have the lock, but push his part of code onto the stack, which is not acceptable and will cause problems.
